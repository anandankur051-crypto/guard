1. Customer Identification Procedure
The company shall obtain the customer's PAN card and address proof at the time of account opening. A photograph of the customer shall be collected and verified against the submitted identity document.

2. Periodic KYC Updation
Customers classified as low risk shall have their KYC records updated once every ten years. Customers classified as high risk shall have their KYC records updated once every two years.

3. Video-based Customer Identification
Video-based customer identification is not currently supported by the company. All KYC must be completed through in-person verification at a branch.

4. Beneficial Ownership Declaration
For non-individual customers such as companies and trusts, the company shall obtain a declaration of the beneficial owner holding twenty-five percent or more ownership or control.

5. Record Retention
All KYC documents and transaction records shall be retained for a minimum period of five years from the date of transaction or account closure, whichever is later.

6. Risk Categorization
Customers shall be categorized into low, medium, and high risk based on the nature of business, location, and transaction profile at the time of account opening only.

7. Politically Exposed Persons
The company shall obtain senior management approval before establishing a relationship with a Politically Exposed Person (PEP) or their family members.
